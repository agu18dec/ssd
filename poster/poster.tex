    \begin{block}{TL;DR}
      \large\raggedright
      To internalize a document, \textbf{self-distillation} trains a model to match itself with the document in context.\\[0.5em]
      \ssd{} lets the student write each rollout and hands over to the teacher \textbf{only at tokens where the two
      disagree}.\\[0.5em]
      It matches \on{} accuracy with \textbf{45\% fewer supervised tokens} and \textbf{forgets less}.
    \end{block}
